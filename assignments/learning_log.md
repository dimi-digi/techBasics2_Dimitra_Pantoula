# Week 2
During the lecture of week 2, we experimented with a breadboard and its power rails. The different colors of the resistors and their meaning was introduced as well. Basically, the resistor color code assists the user to identify and select resistance and tolerance value depending on his project. Important units such as Voltage, Current and Resistance and their relations (Ohm's Law) were briefly explained. Useful terms of electrical current physics were highlighted, for example, that the most common resistance to use was 220Ω, as it limits the current just enough, there is a distinction between direct an alternating current (DC/AC) and 15mA is a safe current for common LEDs. In my opnion, the most interesting, but still not completely comprehensible part of the exercise was the experimentation with series and parallel circuit. There ae specific circumstances when we use each one, e.g. series circuit is used when we need to divide voltage. We acquired new knowledge on coding in Arduino and were introduced to analogWrite(), through which we could manipulate not only the blinking speed but also the RGB values of the LED itsself
